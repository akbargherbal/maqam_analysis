# Notice

This is a personal, non-commercial research project. No audio is stored in the
repository; it holds derived text metadata (ABC hashes, analysis evidence,
maqam labels) for a personal study of the corpus.

- The ABC transcripts were produced with
  [`m-a-p/SheetSage2`](https://huggingface.co/m-a-p/SheetSage2), whose model and
  MERT-v2-FullSong backbone are **CC-BY-NC-4.0** (non-commercial).
- The labels are weak, model-assisted readings, not ground truth, and should
  not be presented as definitive maqam attributions.
- Audio and full ABC transcriptions are not part of this repository; they stay
  in the project's own storage.
